## Status rules

- **Open**: official application window is active.
- **Opens soon**: an official opening date is known and near.
- **Upcoming**: an official future window or schedule exists.
- **Watch**: eligibility/program relevance is established, but the new cycle's official timetable is not yet published.
- **Closed**: the relevant application route has closed.
- **Ineligible**: current known eligibility rules block this cycle.

The UI derives deadline countdowns from the current browser date. It does **not** invent dates for Watch items.
