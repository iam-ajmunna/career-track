## Scholarship lifecycle: Archive, Trash and Deadline History

The site is currently a browser-local workspace. All scholarship views share a canonical lifecycle keyed by programme ID, including an exact alias for Oxford Clarendon. Cambridge general funding and Gates Cambridge remain separate, since they are not identical opportunities. The main scholarship list, screened-out suggestions, Personalized Goal, pipeline, overview, countdowns and exported personal calendar all honour suppression.

- **Archive:** hide a recommendation everywhere. Restore from **Archive & History**. Application checklists remain intact.
- **Delete:** move to Trash immediately. Restore within 10 × 24 hours. After the deadline the stored checklist/application status is erased, Trash no longer exposes the record, and an ID-only tombstone prevents the static catalogue from repopulating it.
- **Deadline expiry:** verified exact timestamps take precedence over date-only notices. Where a closing timezone is unverified, date-only deadlines are conservatively considered expired only after the listed date has passed worldwide (UTC-12); this avoids erasing a still-open application early. Expired scholarships disappear everywhere except **Deadline History**, which retains only programme name, closing date/timestamp and expiry-record timestamp. Full application progress is cleared; expiry cannot be reversed.
- Manual deletion takes precedence over automatic expiry during the ten-day recovery window. A past-deadline item cannot be restored.
- Housekeeping occurs at site startup, when browser storage is reloaded, and while the site remains open. Without an online backend, it cannot run on a closed device. This is a **local workspace**, not cross-device account syncing or cryptographic erasure of the bundled static source catalogue. For portability, use Export progress; clearing browser data removes the recovery/history/tombstone state.
- Static source data and the bundled /deadlines.ics continue to exist in the deployed build; the visible dashboard and its *personalized calendar export* enforce the user's lifecycle state. No external source or other website is changed by local deletion.
