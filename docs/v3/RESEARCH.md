This is measurable initial coverage, not a claim that all global scholarships were found.

Current source records carry a manual verification-session timestamp. Inherited discovery links retain their earlier date-only snapshot provenance. Field claims indicate uncertainty independently; checked funding does not imply a verified application opening. Exact unobserved publication dates, admissions probabilities, visa outcomes, exchange rates and future award amounts are never invented.

Two public Reddit firsthand accounts were inspected and narrowly paraphrased: a UBC MSc funding-with-admission report and a Melbourne PhD budgeting account. Programme/nationality/date limitations are visible. They do not establish official rules. A Facebook Gates video could not be accessed; an embedded official scholar video had no reviewed transcript. No private groups were scraped or inaccessible media summarised. Most route-specific student-experience coverage remains a stated gap.

## Scheduled review

`.github/workflows/source-review.yml` runs daily at 03:23 UTC and supports manual dispatch. It checks approved official/department sources, reads robots rules, rate-limits hosts, rejects unapproved/private destinations and redirects, bounds response sizes and records errors. State is cached for comparison; a 30-day report artifact records initial/unchanged/changed/blocked/error/skipped results. PDFs and inaccessible pages require manual review. No unattended scholarship facts are rewritten. Source fingerprints are noisy change candidates, not semantic verification.

For each changed source: inspect the official page, determine the affected intake/field, reconcile conflicting primary sources, update the individual claim and deadline precision, record a reviewed change, run schema/domain/browser checks, then publish through a reviewed PR. Open/near-deadline calls receive priority. Immigration must be rechecked whenever a real decision depends on it.
